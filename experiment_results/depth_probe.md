# Depth probe (Spatial-VRD, valid split)

`python scripts/depth_probe.py` (MLP, hidden 512) and `--hidden 0` (linear). Pairs of objects of the same image; train on
train images (10% of them held out for early stopping), report on valid; test never read. Mean +- std over seeds 0, 1, 2.
Top-1 accuracy. `rel52`: 52-way relation classification (correct if the top-1 is any true relation of the pair);
`front`: in front of vs behind; `vertical`: above/over vs under; `horizontal`: left of vs right of.
